# Marketing

Marketing owns clear positioning, distribution experiments and the accuracy of
public claims. The starting proposition is: keep agent skills useful as code,
harnesses and working practices change.

The comparison to an addon manager is a positioning hypothesis from the founder
brief. Test whether it helps developers understand the maintenance job. Do not
present it as evidence of market size or demand.

Read [the vision](../vision.md), [roadmap](../product/roadmap.md) and
[claim register](../evidence/claims.json). Current supported product statements
concern local inventory, reversible selected moves, structural linting and
quarantine. Claims about task lift, universal harness behavior or continuous
autonomous repair need their own evidence.

Initial distribution candidates are a reproducible skill-audit example and a
short walkthrough of one accepted drift repair. Publish only redacted, reviewed
artifacts. A proposed post should include its audience, source links, claim IDs,
call to action and the result that will determine whether to repeat it.

The marketing worker drafts inside `marketing/`. It cannot publish, contact
people, spend advertising money or treat an unreviewed draft as an announcement.
Handoff requests can ask product for audience evidence or engineering for a
reproducible measurement. Founder review controls publication.
