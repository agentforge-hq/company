# Product

Product owns the definition of the user's job, the smallest useful scope and
the evidence that determines whether we continue, change or stop.

Current job: help a developer maintain an installed skill collection across
agent harnesses without losing useful instructions or introducing hidden changes.

Read [the vision](../vision.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md),
[v0.1 contract gaps](v0.1-contract-gaps.md) and [evidence register](../evidence/claims.json).
The [source brief](source-brief.md) preserves the founder-supplied proposal;
its research claims and suggested flags require verification.

Product proposals should state the beneficiary, trigger, observable outcome,
scope removed, acceptance check and evidence that would falsify the proposal.
Use the work queue to request engineering feasibility, design review or marketing
claim validation. Keep implementation details in engineering documents.

The product worker proposes changes inside `product/`. It can propose a company
vision revision as a founder decision request, but cannot directly edit or
approve the vision. Close the contract gaps before calling the supplied brief
fully implemented.
